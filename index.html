    How this is calculated expand_more

    The Cumulative Weighted Average (CWA) is calculated by multiplying the score obtained in each course by its credit hours, summing those products, and dividing by the total credit hours.

    CWA = Σ(Score × Credits) / Σ(Credits)

    To find the average this semester needs, your target cumulative CWA and total credits (prior + this semester) determine the total points required — minus the points your prior CWA already contributes — then that's spread across this semester's credit hours.

    Each plan splits that required average differently across courses: the Balanced Plan weights by credit hours, the Equal Split Plan uses the same target everywhere, and the Safety Plan caps every course at a conservative ceiling.
